Practice Sheets 📝

Improve your line quality with these downloadable line width variation practice sheets. For double the practice, draw in pencil first, then ink over your underdrawings.

Digital Artists 🖥️

  1. In the drawing/painting program of your choice create a 2550 pixels by 3300 pixels canvas.
  2. Copy and Paste a given practice sheet as a seperate layer to draw over while trying to match the thickness over the grey line stroke.

Traditional Artists 🧑‍🎨

  1. Print this sheet.
  2. Draw over a given linke stroke while trying to match the thickness over the grey line stroke.
