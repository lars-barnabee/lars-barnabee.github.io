Follow my art journey ✍️ here 😃

larsbarnabee.com is now lars-barnabee.github.io I believe it was time for me to do this. Why? I found it easier for me to use Github pages instead. It became incredibly annoying for me to maintain a website. Dealing with server quirks. My website doesn't get that much traffic either. I am more popular on YouTube. Websites hurt with any traffic these days. I still blog here on occasion.

I am Lars Barnabee 🐝 and I will always be learning to draw. I try to write information and make videos to share what I learned while drawing. I am also a bit of a software engineer, and I make apps that I think are needed by digital artists. I originally made these apps for myself, but I went to great lengths to make it for every artist. I failed drawing in 2018 and came back from my burnt out ashes in 2019 to try again.

To give a timeframe, I started drawabox.com lesson one in September of 2019 and completed it in August of 2021. Starting out, drawabox.com helped me to think in 3D. You can check out my Drawabox Profile Seeing the drawabox course to completion was a big goal of mine. I made a review of the course and it can be found by watching my Drawabox 📦 Review here *Opens in a new tab via YouTube.

I did nothing but drawabox exercises to the point that I stopped drawing for fun to complete the course. Please try to balance practice and fun, the two can even go hand in hand. I am also the creator of Drawesome 🎞️ which is a slideshow application to help those who draw Download Drawesome 🎞️ Here.

Interested in following what I do? Sign up here 📧 for updates on my work.
